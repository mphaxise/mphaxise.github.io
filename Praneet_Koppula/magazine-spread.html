Practice

Praneet Koppula is a product design leader with 18+ years making expert-level engineering tools, AI interactions, and complex systems feel intuitive.

Based in San Francisco, Praneet's career spans field research in India (Intel Classmate PC, Nokia wayfinding, mobile money financial inclusion), engineering tools and design systems at MathWorks, AI interaction vision, and independent experiments.

The common thread: making invisible system state legible, earning trust through user control, and scaling quality through systems that amplify good judgment rather than enforce sameness.

Work spans four countries, 120+ products in a governance ecosystem, and domains from AI-assisted engineering to children's storytelling apps to civic technology research.
