# Minie Story Journey Breakdown Prototype

State: exploration prototype. Production deployment and measured results remain outside its scope.

## Portfolio framing

This interaction-design prototype translates qualitative conversation-log research into a product-facing journey model. It keeps the intended story experience visible, shows where recurring breakdown patterns diverge, and connects each pattern to evidence boundaries and a concrete design opportunity.

The selected portfolio version is a public-safe derivative. Session identifiers, timestamps, direct child-research quotations, story titles, and language-specific details were removed or replaced with composite examples. The internal source remains in the local-only evidence packet and must not be published.

## What the prototype demonstrates

- Progressive disclosure: one stable happy path with selectable breakdown patterns.
- Traceability: observed behavior, unresolved evidence, and opportunity are kept distinct.
- Product synthesis: qualitative findings are translated into actionable journey-stage hypotheses, with prevalence kept outside their scope.
- Responsive interaction: the journey reflows from horizontal desktop lanes to vertical mobile sequences.
