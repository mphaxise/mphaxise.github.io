MATLAB Coder

Testing comprehension in a programmatic interface

Programmatic workflows create a distinct research problem: users must understand names, command structure, typed inputs, generated artifacts, and error recovery. I worked directly with a team member to design a LEGO-style API usability task for MATLAB Coder.

Usability study design API research Task construction Technical workflows
  • Used meaningful command pieces to test conceptual understanding without requiring syntax memorization.
  • Focused the task on whether participants could construct and reason about a command, not merely complete a scripted path.
  • Supported a broader team research context covering GUI and command-line workflows. I do not claim authorship of studies conducted by team members.

MathWorks research and workflow system

Making customer evidence usable across a product ecosystem

I helped build a portfolio-level operating system that connected research and customer evidence with workflow strategy and product planning. The work spanned roughly 120 products and involved researchers, designers, product managers, engineers, quality teams, documentation, marketing, and senior stakeholders.

Research strategy Workflow research Governance Executive synthesis
  • Helped define shared workflow language so teams could compare customer journeys across products and releases.
  • Helped establish Top-N workflow priorities, ownership mechanisms, and advisory communication.
  • Guided teams in connecting qualitative findings, customer requests, product defects, and support signals to planning.
  • Led the system and developed people around it; individual studies remained attributable to the researchers who conducted them.

Turning scattered history into usable evidence

For the Multicultural Arts Center, I initiated and led a public-history research project that brought together archives, institutional records, expert interviews, and community memory. The work supported 50th-anniversary planning and a clearer account of the center's place in East Cambridge.

Archival research Stakeholder interviews Source synthesis Public storytelling
  • Personally researched public archives, organizational records, building history, and Cambridge cultural history.
  • Interviewed historians, institutional leaders, former employees, and archival practitioners.
  • Produced research memos, interview summaries, timeline materials, and narrative assets.
  • Kept source material, interpretation, and public-facing claims distinct.
